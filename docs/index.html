What these videos measure

This is reference-conditioned tracking, not text-to-motion generation. Text labels describe the references; props and finger articulation are not simulated. Videos show the full motion at 25 fps, with 50 Hz control, zero initial velocity and no trajectory post-alignment.

All 128 validation motions: mean per-clip joint RMSE 0.1128 rad, root-position RMSE 0.2046 m, and 128/128 without crossing height/tilt failure thresholds. Completion does not imply perfect tracking. These six examples were selected for presentation and do not replace the full-split evaluation.
